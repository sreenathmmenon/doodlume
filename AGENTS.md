# Doodlume — standing product target

The owner’s explicit target is to win first prize at the Meta hackathon by building a product they consider **100× better than existing alternatives**, with an experience and demo compelling enough to wow **Mark Zuckerberg and Alexandr Wang**. Participation alone is not the target. Preserve this ambition across future sessions and handoffs.

Use that target to guide game design, original creative control, visual quality, interaction, responsiveness, reliability and the final demonstration. Make Meta’s contribution real and demonstrable. The product must work on genuine new inputs, rather than substituting hardcoded scenes or model results. Judge the complete experience, not merely the number of features or passing tests.

“100×” is the owner’s quality ambition, not an established comparison or a guarantee of winning. Measure concrete improvements and report remaining weaknesses honestly. Zuckerberg and Wang are intended audience references, not people who have reviewed or endorsed the project.
