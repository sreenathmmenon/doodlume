## Current evidence limits

New images can require correction, and provider stalls remain possible. Photo resource transfer is game logic, not physical liquid simulation. Save data is browser-local with portable backups. Passing tests, raw footage and a narration plan do not establish universal image support, measured user retention or a final judging score.

The full authenticated guide capture stays in ignored local research output. No participant chats, credentials, private account details or personal source paths are included in the public-facing documentation.
